Plant care made simple

Use these quick guides to understand light, watering, soil and common warning signs.

On this pageWateringSunlightSoil & FeedingTroubleshootingQuick Care TablePractical Care VideosTrusted Resources

Watering without guesswork

Check the soil before watering. For most houseplants, water only when the top few centimetres feel dry. Empty excess water from saucers so roots do not sit in water.

  • Use room-temperature water when possible.
  • Water less often in cooler months.
  • Plants in bright light usually dry faster.

Reading your light

Bright indirect light means a bright room without harsh midday sun directly hitting the leaves. Low-light plants tolerate dimmer spaces, but no plant can thrive in complete darkness.

Soil and feeding

Use a well-draining potting mix and a pot with drainage holes. Feed actively growing plants lightly during spring and summer, following the fertiliser label.

Common warning signs

  • Yellow leaves: often too much water, poor drainage or normal ageing.
  • Brown edges: can indicate dry air, underwatering or salt build-up.
  • Long, weak growth: usually a sign that the plant needs more light.
